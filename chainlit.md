1. 🔍 Search Tavily + X.com for trending AI topics
2. 🔄 Check deduplication against your past posts
3. 📚 Retrieve relevant context from the AIE9 knowledge base
4. ✍️ Generate a Hook → Analogy → Tech Concept → CTA LinkedIn post
5. 🎨 Generate a poster image (DALL-E 3)

**Knowledge Chat** — ask any question:
- Chat with the AIE9 course knowledge base + your past posts
- Answers grounded in course material with personalized analogies
- Knowledge Graph traversal for richer, connected context
